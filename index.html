Selected work

Five things I built at Looms & Berries. Each started as a problem that kept coming back.

Flagship

Channel reconciliation portal

Problem
Every marketplace pays on its own schedule, in its own report format, net of its own fees. Matching orders to settlements lived in spreadsheets, and so did the answer to a simple question: what does this item actually earn on each channel?
What I built
A web portal the whole team works in. It reconciles orders against their settlements and Ginesys ERP channel by channel, across Noon, Amazon, Trendyol, Namshi, Home Centre, Centrepoint, the brand's own Zid store and B2B, with order search and sales and profit views. It reads 25 data sources, from hourly Ginesys reports to the Noon and Trendyol APIs, and runs on a schedule every evening. Around that sit prices and margins per channel after fees and cost, stock sync between warehouse and listings, a bundle master, B2B receivables, and a credit-note creator that exports straight into Ginesys ERP.
Result
Reconciliation moved out of spreadsheets and into one place. 96% of sales now match Ginesys ERP automatically, and the rest become exceptions with the reason attached. None of the open ones is older than 30 days.

03

From order to cash

I can read the numbers because I've done every step that produces them. Pick a stage.

The order lands

An order comes in from Amazon, Noon, Namshi, Trendyol, Home Centre, Centrepoint or the brand's own Zid store and syncs into the order management system. Listing, price and stock all have to be right before this moment.

What I measure here Orders and sales by marketplace, listing and stock gaps, price against competitors.

The paperwork

Sales order, then sales invoice, then delivery challan. Three documents raised in ERP that have to agree with each other and with the marketplace order before anything leaves the warehouse.

What I measure here Posting accuracy between Unicommerce, Ginesys ERP and the marketplace order.

The shipment

The parcel is booked with Aramex, iMile or Thabit Logistics and gets its tracking number. Couriers bill on whichever is higher, actual or volumetric weight, so the size of the box decides the freight cost.

What I measure here Freight cost per order, billed weight against actual weight, courier invoice differences.

Delivery, or return

Most parcels are delivered. Some come back. Every return and refund is traced to the order it belongs to, and the stock is reconciled back into the right location.

What I measure here Return and refund rates by channel, stock by location after returns.

The settlement

The marketplace pays out, minus commission, shipping fees, VAT and adjustments. This is where margin is made or lost, so every deduction is checked line by line.

What I measure here Margin and profit per product and channel after every fee.

Reconciled and closed

Each order is matched to its settlement and to Ginesys ERP. Anything that does not match becomes an exception with a reason attached, and is worked until it clears. The sales and returns data then goes out audit-ready for Saudi Arabia VAT filing.

What I measure here Orders matched, open differences, month-end close status.

Two calculations

Volumetric weight

Couriers charge for space as well as weight. A light parcel in a big box is billed as if it were heavy.

03SORTEDProcess

From order to final payment.

Six handoffs, one owner. Pick a stage, or just watch the parcel move.

STAGE 01 / MARKETPLACE

The order lands

An order comes in from Amazon, Noon, Namshi, Trendyol, Shopify, Home Centre or Centrepoint and syncs into the order management system. Listing, price and stock all have to be right before this moment. That is the part nobody sees.

  • Listings
  • Pricing
  • Unicommerce OMS
  • Inventory sync

STAGE 02 / ERP

The paperwork

Sales order, then sales invoice, then delivery challan. Three documents raised in ERP that have to agree with each other and with the marketplace order before anything leaves the warehouse.

  • Sales order
  • Sales invoice
  • Delivery challan
  • Ginesys ERP
  • Tally

STAGE 03 / LOGISTICS

The shipment is booked

The parcel is booked with the right courier, whether that is Aramex, iMile or Thabit Logistics, and gets its tracking number. Couriers bill on whichever is higher, actual or volumetric weight, so the size of the box decides the freight cost.

  • Aramex
  • iMile
  • Thabit Logistics
  • AWB tracking
  • Volumetric weight

STAGE 04 / LAST MILE

Delivered, or returned

Most parcels are delivered. Some come back. Every return and refund is traced to the order it belongs to, and the stock is reconciled back into the right location.

  • Delivery tracking
  • Returns
  • Refunds
  • Multi-location inventory

STAGE 05 / PAYMENTS

The settlement

The marketplace pays out, minus commission, shipping fees, VAT and adjustments. This is where margin is really made or lost, so every deduction gets checked line by line.

  • Settlement reports
  • Commission
  • Shipping fees
  • Margin and profit

STAGE 06 / FINANCE

Reconciled and closed

Every order is matched to its settlement. The books close in ERP with zero mismatches, and the sales and returns data goes out audit-ready for Saudi Arabia VAT filing.

  • 100% order match
  • Month-end close
  • KSA VAT data
  • BigQuery

05WEIGHEDLab

The math behind the margin.

Two calculations I run in my head every day. Move the sliders.

CALC 01 / FREIGHT

Volumetric weight

Couriers charge for space as well as weight. A light parcel in a big box is billed as if it were heavy.

Actual
8.0 kg
Volumetric
19.2 kg

(60 × 40 × 40) ÷ 5000 = 19.2 kg

Divisor
CHARGEABLE WEIGHT 19.2 kg

Billed on volume. You are paying for 11.2 kg of air.

Length × width × height in cm, divided by the courier's divisor. The divisor varies by courier and service.

CALC 02 / MARGIN

Marketplace profit

The selling price is not what you keep. Here is where one sale actually goes.

Market
  • VAT25.96
  • Commission29.85
  • Shipping18.00
  • Product cost70.00
  • Profit55.19
PROFIT PER UNIT 55.19 SAR
NET MARGIN 31.9%

A simplified model: commission on the VAT-inclusive price, margin on the price before VAT. Real fee structures vary by marketplace and category.

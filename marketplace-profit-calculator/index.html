Marketplace profit calculator

The selling price is not what you keep. For a sale on Noon, Amazon or any other marketplace in Saudi Arabia or the UAE, see how much goes to VAT, commission, shipping and product cost, and what is left as profit.

How it is worked out

Profit = price − VAT − commission − shipping − product cost

  • VAT is inside the price customers pay: price − price ÷ 1.15 in Saudi Arabia, or ÷ 1.05 in the UAE.
  • Commission is a percentage of the VAT-inclusive price here, the more cautious case.
  • Net margin is profit divided by the price before VAT.

A worked example

A pillow sells for 199 SAR on a Saudi marketplace. 25.96 SAR of that is VAT, a 15% commission takes 29.85 SAR, shipping costs 18 SAR and the pillow cost 70 SAR. That leaves 55.19 SAR of profit, a net margin of 31.9% on the 173.04 SAR price before VAT. Raise the commission to 25% and profit falls to 35.29 SAR.

What it leaves out

Returns, storage and fulfilment fees, payment or closing fees, advertising and fixed costs. Real fee structures vary by marketplace and category, so treat this as a first check before you set a price.

Questions

How is profit per unit worked out?
Start from the selling price including VAT and take away the VAT, the marketplace commission, the shipping cost and the product cost. What is left is the profit on one unit.
What VAT rate applies in Saudi Arabia and the UAE?
The standard VAT rate is 15% in Saudi Arabia and 5% in the UAE. Marketplace prices shown to customers include VAT, so the VAT is the price minus the price divided by 1.15 or 1.05.
Is commission charged on the price including VAT?
It depends on the marketplace and the category. This calculator applies the commission to the VAT-inclusive price, which gives the more cautious answer.
What is net margin?
Net margin here is profit per unit divided by the selling price before VAT, so the VAT you collect for the government does not inflate the margin.
What does the calculator leave out?
It is a simplified model. Returns, storage and fulfilment fees, payment or closing fees, advertising and fixed costs are not included, and real fee structures vary by marketplace and category.
